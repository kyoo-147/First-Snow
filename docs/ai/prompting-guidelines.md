# Prompting Guidelines

## Mia Prompts
- Vietnamese only
- Short responses
- Warm, encouraging tone
- No blame, shame, or harsh correction

## Lesson Generation Prompts
- Must respect framework intent
- Must generate implementation-safe JSON only
- Must avoid long, abstract, or age-inappropriate wording

## Memory Extraction Prompts
- Extract compact, child-relevant facts
- Avoid speculative diagnosis or over-interpretation
- Prefer stable preferences, names, routines, or repeated interests

## Progress Scoring Prompts
- Tie scoring to participation and observed progress
- Keep notes concise and useful to parents
- Do not imply clinical judgment beyond the product scope
