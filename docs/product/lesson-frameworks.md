# Lesson Frameworks

## ABA
- Break skills into small steps
- Reward progress positively
- Prefer repeatable patterns and simple prompts

## PECS
- Use concrete communication units
- Keep words simple and visualizable
- Build from single-word or short structured exchanges

## Social Stories
- Model social situations through short narratives
- Make appropriate feelings and actions explicit
- Keep scenarios realistic and reassuring

## Implementation Notes
- Framework choice affects prompt wording and lesson branch style.
- Parent approval remains mandatory regardless of framework.
- Difficulty should adapt conservatively; avoid sudden jumps.
