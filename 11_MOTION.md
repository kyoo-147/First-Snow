# Snow Motion Rules

## Motion principle

Motion should make Snow feel alive, not distracting.

## Companion animation states

### Idle

- gentle breathing
- slow blink
- small scarf movement

### Listening

- mic pulse
- waveform animation
- attentive eyes
- slight lean forward

### Thinking

- small snowflake loop
- looking upward
- soft sparkle near head

### Speaking

- mouth movement
- subtitle text appears
- waveform active

### Encouraging

- wave
- heart or star reaction
- short celebration

## Motion intensity

Child default: low to medium.
Rewards page may use more celebration, but no intense flashing.

## Durations

```txt
Micro interactions: 120ms to 180ms
Card transitions: 200ms to 300ms
Companion idle loop: 3s to 6s
Celebration: under 1.5s
```
