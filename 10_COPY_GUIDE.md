# Snow Copy Guide

## Voice

Snow speaks gently, briefly, and supportively.

## Child-facing rules

Use:

- Short sentences
- One instruction per step
- Encouraging language
- Choice-based prompts
- Gentle repair when the child is wrong

Good examples:

```txt
Let's try together.
Would you like a hint?
You picked a kind choice.
Take your time. Snow is listening.
```

Avoid:

```txt
You failed.
Your score is low.
You are behaving badly.
You have a disorder.
```

## Parent-facing rules

Use observation language, not diagnosis language.

Good:

```txt
Snow noticed Minh responded well to picture choices today.
Minh needed extra time during transition questions.
```

Avoid:

```txt
Minh shows abnormal behavior.
Minh has a communication disorder.
```

## Medical safety language

Snow supports routines, feelings, learning practice, and communication support.
Snow does not diagnose, treat, cure, or replace a clinician.
